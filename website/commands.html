Idle Hunter / Field guide

Commands

Find the command you need. Use these inside Discord, in a server where Idle Hunter is available.

/menu

Open the main hunter menu

/profile

View your hunter profile (or another player's)

/hunt

Go hunting in your current biome!

/progression

View your achievements, badges, and titles

/events

View ongoing global events

/shop

Buy boosts, tools and ammo

/biome

Choose your hunting biome

/color

Change your color for containers

/equip

Equip your tools, ammo and vehicles

/idle

Manage your passive Hunting Camp

/daily

Claim your daily reward

/prestige

Reset for a permanent boost multiplier

/mail

Check your mailbox

/quests

View and claim your daily quests

/tribe menu

View your tribe and its options

/tribe create

Create a new tribe

/tribe invite

Invite a player to your tribe

/tribe leave

Leave your current tribe

/tribe info

View info about a tribe

/leaderboard

View hunter and tribe leaderboards

/record

View a hunter's catch record book

/log

View your recent hunt log

/gift

Gift money or gems to another player

/verify

Verify you're not an autoclicker!

/invite

Invite Idle Hunter to your server!

/id

Get a user's Discord ID

/help

View all available commands

/craft

Fuse shards into crystals and buy Hunting Crates

/use

Open or use an item you own (e.g. a Hunting Crate)

/rules

View the Idle Hunter rules

/lottery

Buy tickets for the daily lottery draw

/gamble

Try your luck at various games

/suggest

Make a suggestion for Idle Hunter

/report

Report a user or a bug

/tutorial

Turn tutorial tips on or off
