Tribes

Join or lead a tribe to share boosts, chase weekly contracts, and climb the tribe leaderboard together. Use /tribe create or /tribe menu in Discord to get started.

Roles

Every tribe has one Leader, any number of Officers and Members, and Recruits on a 24-hour probation before they're promoted to full Member.

Leader

Full control — invite, kick, promote, set the description, and disband.

Officer

Trusted help — invite and kick members, run the Perk Shop and Contracts.

🧑

Member

Full participation — hunts, quests and dailies all feed the tribe's XP.

🌱

Recruit

New joiners. Auto-promoted to Member after 24 hours in the tribe.

Progression

Tribes level up from banked Tribe XP. Leveling raises the member cap and unlocks new features, up to the level cap of 30.

Weekly Contracts

Unlocked at Tribe Level 2. Three shared goals reset every week — everyone's contribution counts toward the same bar.

Combined Haul

Catch animals as a tribe — the target scales with your member count.

Spread Out

Hunt across 3–5 different biomes as a tribe this week.

All Hands

Get enough members to each complete 2 personal tasks this week.

Expeditions

Unlocked at Tribe Level 8. Vote on a route as a group, then fill a shared progress bar with hunts, catches, myth kills, dailies and quests before time runs out.
