§ 02 — Getting started

From nothing
to your first haul.

01

Open the menu

In a server with Idle Hunter, type /menu. Use /help whenever you need the full command list.

02

Go on a hunt

Use /hunt to catch creatures in your current biome. Sell your catches through the hunt menu to earn coins.

03

Build your loadout

Visit /shop, then use /equip to choose your tools, compatible ammo, and vehicles.

Want Idle Hunter in your own server? Run /invite where the bot is available, then follow Discord’s authorization flow. You’ll need permission to add apps to the destination server.

§ 03 — Good to know

A little help for the trail.

Where do I play?

Idle Hunter runs inside Discord. This website is a guide and home for the game’s policies; gameplay happens through the bot’s commands.

Why can’t I hunt again yet?

Hunts have cooldowns. Follow the time shown by the bot. If a verification prompt appears, complete it yourself; don’t use automation to bypass it.

How do tribes work?

Use /tribe create to create a tribe, or ask an existing tribe for an invitation. Check /mail for invites and /tribe menu for tribe options. Some actions require a leader or officer role.

What happens when I prestige?

Prestige resets parts of your progress in exchange for permanent boosts. Read the current reset and reward details in /prestige before confirming.

Can I trade game currency for real money?

No. Coins, gems, and other virtual items are for gameplay and are not redeemable for cash or real-world prizes.

How do I request deletion of my data?

Contact a maintainer through the support server and ask for a private conversation. See the Privacy Policy for the request process. Removing the bot from a server does not automatically delete your game account.

§ 04 — Support & policies

Keep the adventure fair.

Play fairly, respect other hunters, and report issues. Use /rules for the in-game rules or contact the support team when you need help.
